Summer Vacation

Summer holidays are here today,
Books and homework far away.
Wake up late and laugh out loud,
Feeling happy, feeling proud.

Play some cricket in the sun,
Every moment full of fun.
Draw and dance and sing along,
Make each day a happy song.

Ice cream melting, mango sweet,
Running fast on tiny feet.
Friends together every day,
Smiles and laughter all the way.

Summer days go shining bright,
Stars are glowing every night.
Holiday time, hip hooray,
Let's have fun the whole day.
